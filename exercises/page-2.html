Purple Rain

Introduction

Purple Rain is the sixth studio album by the American musician Prince, and the first credited to Prince and The Revolution. It was released by Warner Bros. Records in June 1984 as the soundtrack to the film of the same name.

The album produced several hit singles, including "When Doves Cry", "Let's Go Crazy" and the title track, "Purple Rain".

Recording

Most of the album was recorded in studios in California and Minnesota during 1983 and 1984. Some of the basic tracks, including the title song, came from a live concert Prince gave at the First Avenue nightclub in Minneapolis in August 1983.

Reception

Purple Rain spent many weeks at number one on the US Billboard 200 chart and went on to sell millions of copies worldwide. Prince won an Academy Award for the film's music, and the album is often listed among the greatest albums of all time.
